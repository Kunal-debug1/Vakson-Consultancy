Solar Advisory & Implementation

Comprehensive feasibility studies, ROI modelling, and implementation oversight.

We provide full-scale solar advisory services designed for real industrial load profiles. From rooftop to ground-mount systems, we analyze your consumption patterns, manage net-metering applications, provide subsidy assistance, and oversee the installation and commissioning process to guarantee ROI.

Electricity Bill Auditing & Energy Efficiency

Tariff correction, billing dispute resolution, and load analysis.

Our audit experts review your historical bills to correct tariff misclassifications and handle billing disputes directly with the electricity board. We also conduct full-site load analysis to create utility-wide cost-saving strategies that optimize your entire facility's energy consumption.

HVAC System Design & Optimization

Design validation, performance tuning and retrofits.

We optimize industrial-scale HVAC installations through rigorous design validation and performance tuning. Our engineers assess your current systems for inefficiencies and design retrofits that improve climate control while significantly reducing energy expenditure.

Electrical & Automation Systems

Control panels, switchgear, energy meters and PLC/SCADA systems.

Our electrical and automation services cover the specification and commissioning of essential industrial power hardware. From advanced energy meters to full PLC/SCADA integration, we ensure your electrical infrastructure is robust, responsive, and reliable.

MEP Design & BIM Validation

BIM-based clash detection and validation for MEP systems.

We audit mechanical, electrical, and plumbing designs using Building Information Modeling (BIM). Our clash detection and validation processes ensure that complex utility systems integrate perfectly before construction begins, saving both time and money.

Solar Advisory & Implementation — From Feasibility to Commissioning

Navigating the transition to solar energy requires more than just installing panels on a roof. It requires a deep understanding of your facility's energy consumption patterns, structural constraints, and local regulatory environments. At Vakson, we provide comprehensive solar advisory services designed specifically for industrial and commercial facilities.

  • Complete feasibility study with ROI projections: We analyze your load profile before recommending any system size.
  • Net-metering setup and subsidy documentation: We handle all the paperwork with state electricity boards.
  • Installation oversight and commissioning support: We manage the EPC contractors to ensure quality and compliance.

Our process begins with an in-depth feasibility study to determine the optimal system size and technology for your specific load profile. We don't believe in oversized, sales-driven proposals; we recommend what makes engineering and economic sense for your plant.

Once a design is approved, our team handles the complex net-metering application process and ensures that all documentation is perfectly aligned with state electricity board requirements. We then oversee the installation, conducting rigorous quality checks until final commissioning and sign-off, ensuring your investment yields maximum returns from day one.

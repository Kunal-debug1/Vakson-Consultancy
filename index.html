    About Vakson

    Vakson Consultancy Services is a technical consultancy built on one idea: energy systems should be measured, modelled and proven before a rupee is spent.

    • In-house feasibility studies, load analysis and compliance documentation
    • Solar and energy solutions sized for real industrial load profiles
    • ROI-first recommendations — savings and system longevity over quick installs

    For over 25 years we have helped industrial plants, manufacturers and institutions plan their shift to solar, correct their electricity bills, and tune the electrical, HVAC and utility systems running underneath it all. Our turnkey delivery covers full EPC coordination, from start to commissioning.
